Application checklist

What to confirm before selecting.

A completed list is useful but does not confirm product suitability. Review current manufacturer documentation and project specifications for the exact model, configuration and service.

  • Fluid composition, state, cleanliness, concentration and process purpose.
  • Normal, minimum, maximum and design operating pressure and temperature.
  • Nominal pipe size, end connection, face-to-face and installation orientation.
  • Body, trim, seat, stem and sealing material requirements for actual service.
  • Flow duty, pressure drop, shut-off requirements and actuation/control needs.
  • Applicable standards, approved vendor list and project-specific documents.
  • Destination country, quantity, requested date and inspection documentation.

Common questions

Confirm before you commit.

Always validate each product against its approved manufacturer's data and project specification.

Where are verified product datasheets?

No ARSCON product datasheets are provided or approved for this website yet. Request the current manufacturer datasheet for the exact proposed model, materials, pressure class and configuration before approval.

Can this website confirm pressure or temperature ratings?

No. Ratings depend on the specific model, materials, size, pressure class, configuration, service and manufacturer's operating limits. Confirm those details from current approved documentation.

Does a search result confirm availability or delivery?

No. Catalogue search results are not a live inventory, quotation or delivery promise. Ask ARSCON to confirm availability, price, currency, lead time and delivery terms in writing.

Who approves safety-critical selection?

A qualified engineer with access to the actual duty, applicable standards and current manufacturer information must approve the selection and its suitability.

How can a non-technical buyer start an enquiry?

Share the application, fluid, operating conditions, size, connection, material preferences, destination and quantity. The preliminary assistant can highlight missing fields but cannot approve a product.
