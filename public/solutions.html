A useful enquiry

Details that make the first conversation count.

Good inputs help the procurement team understand the application. They do not guarantee that a particular product, service, schedule or technical outcome can be supplied.

Availability of product selection, sourcing, engineering support, inspection, repair, installation or after-sales work must be confirmed in writing with ARSCON.
01

Describe the service

Identify the fluid, equipment, application, process duty and consequences of an incorrect selection.

02

Share real operating values

State the normal and design pressure and temperature, nominal size, flow requirements and process conditions.

03

Define project constraints

Include material requirements, connections, standards, destination, quantity and required delivery date if known.

04

Review against approved information

Confirm that the current manufacturer datasheet, applicable specifications, commercial terms and availability all match.

05

Get qualified engineering approval

A qualified engineer must verify safety-critical product selection, pressure and temperature limits and application compatibility.
