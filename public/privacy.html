Privacy information

Your information. A careful approach.

This preliminary website notice describes how the present site behaves. ARSCON must review and complete the applicable legal identity, jurisdiction, contact details, retention period and other operational facts before launch.

Draft website notice; legal review required.

This document does not establish a processing address, data controller, cross-border arrangement, retention promise, legal basis or contractual relationship. The business owner must verify those details and obtain appropriate legal review before publication.

Information you enter in an enquiry

The RFQ form requests a business, contact name, work email, optional phone, destination, product requirements, quantity, optional target delivery date and optional project notes. The browser also sends a blank anti-spam honeypot field. You may download a local draft; downloading does not transmit it to ARSCON. Do not enter passwords, payment details, sensitive personal data or commercially sensitive project files.

When information leaves this site

RFQ submissions are accepted only if the website owner configures an HTTPS RFQ webhook. Without it, the server explicitly refuses the submission, shows an error and does not state that the enquiry was delivered. If configured, the webhook service receives the enquiry fields, a timestamp and the source identifier. The destination, data location, operators, safeguards and retention schedule must be independently reviewed before the site accepts live requests.

The optional valve assistant's rule-based mode processes supplied technical inputs on the website server and does not contact an AI provider. If the owner configures a third-party AI API, application specification fields and verified catalogue records are sent only to that configured AI API; personal contact details from the RFQ are not included. Confirm the AI provider's terms, data location, retention and privacy arrangements before enabling this capability. The AI API key remains server-side.

Operational records and security

Normal hosting, network and service-provider systems may process connection, diagnostic, security and error information. This website does not intentionally log enquiry field contents or secret keys in its own application error messages. Hosting provider logs, storage locations and retention settings depend on the deployment and must be reviewed by the owner. Security headers, input checks, body-size limits and simple per-instance request throttling are not a substitute for secure hosting or an operational security review.

Essential browser preference

This site can use local browser storage solely to remember a visitor's essential-only preference. It does not configure analytics, marketing pixels or advertising cookies. Browser functionality may be limited if local storage is unavailable. See the cookie details.

Your requests and contact details

The appropriate way to ask about personal-data rights, deletion, access or correction must be supplied and verified by ARSCON before launch. No privacy email, named controller or postal address is currently configured. Do not submit a privacy request using an unverified contact channel.

Updates and effective date

The business owner should record the effective date, the approved legal entity, applicable local and international privacy requirements and each deployed data processor when this draft is reviewed. Updates should reflect actual configuration and business practice rather than anticipated features.
